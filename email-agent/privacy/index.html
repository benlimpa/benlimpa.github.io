Email Agent

Privacy policy

How the local Email Agent handles Google user data.

Effective September 2, 2026

Overview

Email Agent is a personal command-line tool operated by Ben Limpanukorn. It is used for my own Gmail accounts and is not a hosted service offered to other users.

Google data the tool accesses

When I authorize an account, Email Agent accesses Gmail messages, message metadata, folders, and the account identity needed to synchronize and organize that mailbox. It may move messages between folders and save drafts when explicitly requested. It does not send email or permanently delete messages.

How the data is used

Gmail data is used only to provide the tool's local mailbox mirror, search, preview, and user-directed folder-move features. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or training machine-learning models.

Storage and sharing

Messages and their local search index are stored on my computer in a local Maildir and notmuch database. OAuth client credentials and access tokens are stored locally with encryption. Email Agent has no developer-operated backend, and I do not sell or share Gmail data with third parties. Gmail remains the service used to access the account and is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Retention and control

Local copies remain on the computer until I delete them. I can revoke Email Agent's access from the Google Account security page and remove the local mailbox, index, and token files at any time.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to ben@limpanu.com.
